The SCHATTEN duel screen shows both wizard panels, the cards in your own hand, legal actions, and a running event log. Learn these surfaces before judging a surprising outcome.

## Wizard panels

Each wizard's panel includes **Health / maximum Health**, **Ward**, **Mana / cap / carry**, **Statuses**, **Charge**, **Hand count / limit**, **Deck count**, **Record count**, equipment, and the ordered **Schatten slots**.

Your own hand shows card names and text. Your opponent's hand count is visible but the card identities are hidden during a live duel.

## How to choose actions

- **Mulligan:** keep the hand or tick specific cards to return and redraw. There are two mulligans per wizard.
- **Cast:** offered legal choices are grouped with specific modes, targets and branching choices. Pick a button for the intended action.
- **Veil:** places the card into the leftmost available Schatten slot; wait for fuse or Reaction timing.
- **Dusk:** after selecting End Turn, if over the hand limit, tick discards or use the game's default most-recently-drawn choice.
- **AI turn:** an opponent-is-thinking banner appears while its worker decides.

The game does not ask players to enter arbitrary moves. The interface presents legal actions produced by the rules engine.

## Reading the event log

Compare the log after every cast with the panels. If an effect does not make sense, save the match's Duel Record and identify its step in [verified replay](/replay/record-and-review).

The [turn sequence](/rules/turn-structure) explains when Ward expires, status effects tick and Veils activate.
