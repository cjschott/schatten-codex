The **Weave** system is a central source of tactical depth. A Primer creates a Charge. You can shape that Charge, then spend it through a Release.

## The parts of a Weave

| Card category | Main purpose | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primer | Start a Charge with an element and magnitude M | [Kindle](/cards/kindle) |
| Modifier | Increase the active Charge's magnitude | [Stoke](/cards/stoke) |
| Conduit | Convert the Charge's element | [Prismatic Conduit](/cards/prismatic-conduit) |
| Release | Consume the Charge for a payoff | [Immolate](/cards/immolate) |

The Charge remembers its current element, magnitude **M**, element history, names used, link count and whether it has been carried.

## Key legality rules

- Playing a new Primer when a Charge is already active **gutters** the old Charge and starts a new one.
- A Modifier or Conduit needs an active Charge. The same card name cannot appear twice in the same Weave.
- A Weave must preserve room in the staff's **Loom** for a final Release. Modifier or Conduit links must not exceed **Loom minus 1**; a Release may fill the last link.
- Charge magnitude is capped at **12**. An additive Modifier increases M up to that cap.
- The element may be converted only to a permitted element not already in the Charge history. Conversions use the elemental Wheel and may incur penalties.
- A Release consumes the Charge. Base Release damage is capped at **15**, with further effect and defense processing.

## Prototype elemental riders

These special effects follow the **final Charge element when a Release resolves**:

| Element | Rider |
| --- | --- |
| Fire | Apply Burn equal to floor(M / 3) |
| Frost | Apply 1 Chill if M is at least 3 |
| Arcane | Draw 1 if M is at least 4 |
| Umbra | Release is Unseen at declaration |

Storm, Life and Decay also have defined riders in the general design but **are not part of the current prototype card disciplines**.

## Veiling a Primer

When a veiled Primer resolves, its specified Veil bonus applies. For example, Kindle creates magnitude 4 instead of 3. A pending veiled Primer and a carried Charge cannot be stacked as simultaneous pending Charge sources.

## Carrying a Charge

At Dusk you may pay 1 mana and occupy a free Schatten slot to preserve a Charge for the next Dawn if it was never previously carried and no conflicting pending Charge source exists. It returns at **M + 1**, capped at 12. Otherwise the Charge gutters at turn end.
