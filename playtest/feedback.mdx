Your playtesting helps SCHATTEN improve its core combat experience. The goal is not simply to win: it is to discover which choices feel strategic, which interactions are confusing, and where the interface gets in the way.

## Suggested test route

<Steps>
  <Step title="Try the four presets">
    Play L1 Ember Assault, L2 Rime Control, L3 Gloam Ledger and L4 Prism Weaver at least once each.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare opponent styles">
    Begin with Greedy, then try the provisional default ISMCTS Low. Use Random Legal or ISMCTS High for additional comparisons.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the key mechanics">
    Test mulligans, Manifest versus Veil, fuse timing, reactions, mana carry, Charge, Dusk discards, targets, Ward and status effects.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save each match">
    Download the Duel Record and session file. Reopen them as a verified replay and record exact step numbers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit feedback">
    Use the hosted game's feedback interface. Include the game version, preset, opponent, replay step, what occurred, and what you expected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to report

| Category | Helpful report |
| --- | --- |
| Bug | Exact replay step, actual behavior, expected behavior, and whether reproducible |
| Clarity | Text or control you misunderstood and what interpretation you expected |
| Balance | Repeated match pattern, loadouts, opponents, and supporting duel files |
| Strategy | Combo or counterplay you discovered and why it mattered |
| Feature request | What player problem the requested feature would solve |

Single-session impressions are useful feedback but do not establish statistical balance. If you are unsure what happened, report the confusion rather than claiming a rules defect.

## Privacy and notes

The **Wizard's Journal** stores private notes in your browser, not as synchronized cross-device documents. Downloaded duel files can disclose both sides' hidden information after the match. Keep them private unless you want to share the battle.
