## Publication policy

Content describes **Playable**, **Engine foundation**, **Planned**, or **Under review** behavior. New mechanics do not become official merely because they appear in a future-facing idea or sample. When a game release changes rules, update the affected Codex pages and reference version together.

Player-facing pages avoid deployment internals, account secrets, internal approval reports and infrastructure configuration. See [prototype scope](/start/prototype-status) for what testers can actually play now.
