The prototype playtest should answer whether **the combat itself** is enjoyable, understandable and strategically interesting. Use these exercises instead of relying only on the final winner.

## Suggested exercises

| Scenario | Action | What to observe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Loadout tour | Play each of the four presets | Noticeable strategic differences and deck clarity |
| Opponent comparison | Play Greedy then ISMCTS Low | Predictability, challenge and decision delays |
| Veil versus Manifest | Cast eligible cards both ways | Timing, fuse and bonus clarity |
| Resource decisions | Build a Charge and carry it if legal | Mana carry versus Charge carry confusion |
| Reaction response | Encounter a declared Release or damage Reaction | Whether the counterplay is understandable |
| Status timing | Apply Burn or Chill and watch next Dawn | Correct damage and mana changes |
| Replay audit | Export two files, reopen, verify and step through | Exact and understandable reconstruction |
| Freeform experience | Play a duel without focusing on tests | Fun, frustration, pacing and willingness to replay |

## Report template

```text
SCHATTEN version:
Rules engine:
Loadout:
AI opponent and loadout:
Duel ID:
Replay step or round:
Category: bug / clarity / balance / strategy / feature
Expected:
Observed:
Why it matters:
Can I reproduce it?
Attached: duel.json / session.json (if appropriate)
```

For balance feedback, describe repeated patterns. One result is a **personal experience**, not statistical evidence of imbalance.

For problems you cannot explain, select **clarity** and record what you thought the move should do. That is valuable, even if the engine followed the rules correctly.
