SCHATTEN is a tactical wizard card battler built around a defining idea: **your equipment creates your deck**. Select a loadout, make meaningful casting decisions, and study the duel afterward.

<Note>The current private browser build is a **combat and playtesting prototype**, not a progression or crafting game. Reference: web release `a84f128c5017`, rules-core `0.4.0`.</Note>

## The core philosophy

SCHATTEN should be easy to approach without giving up strategic depth. A player should be able to enjoy one battle without chores or daily obligations, while advanced players can explore counterplay, resource management, and equipment combinations.

The current documentation deliberately distinguishes **playable prototype features** from design intentions. Mechanics and item descriptions will be expanded only after they are verified against the canonical content.
