title: "Mana, refresh, caps, and carry"
description: "Understand SCHATTEN mana progression, the first-player adjustment, carry limits, Chill penalties, and when unspent mana disappears."
---

Mana pays for casting cards. SCHATTEN's baseline `C1` gives each wizard increasing base mana across their own turns, subject to equipment caps.

## Base mana

For your own turn number **t**, base mana is the smaller of `2 + t` and your mana cap. The wizard playing first gets **one less mana only on their first turn**.

| Your own turn | First player base | Second player base |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 2 | 3 |
| 2 | 4 | 4 |
| 3 | 5 | 5 |
| 4 onward | 6 | 6 |

This table assumes the default mana cap of **6**. The Rimeheart Seal can set a lower cap.

## Dawn refresh

Apply these operations in order:

1. Calculate base mana for your own turn number.
2. Add your prior turn's carry (0 or 1).
3. Add refresh bonuses such as Feint's exposure bonus.
4. Subtract all refresh penalties, including Chill stacks.
5. Clamp the resulting pool to at least 1 and at most **mana cap + 2**.
6. Clear the consumed penalties and bonuses.

**Example:** base 6, carry 1, Chill 2 and another penalty of 3 produces `6 + 1 - 2 - 3 = 2` mana.

## Spending and carrying

A cast pays its full adjusted cost. Costs never go below zero. Normal end-of-turn mana carry is **at most 1**. An unused Charge is a *different* resource: carrying a Charge costs 1 mana and occupies a Schatten slot if legal.

A Charge carried this way gains 1 magnitude when it returns at your next Dawn and **cannot be carried again**.

<Warning>Do not confuse mana carry (up to 1 unspent mana) with Charge carry (an active Weave stored as a Schatten object). They have different conditions and consequences.</Warning>
