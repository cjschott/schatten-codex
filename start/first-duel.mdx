Start your first SCHATTEN duel by selecting a validated equipment preset and an AI opponent. The browser build uses the canonical rules engine and offers only legal actions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the private playtest">
    Visit [the SCHATTEN prototype](https://schatten-dev.virrynstudios.com). Access is limited to authorized testers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your loadout">
    Try **L1 Ember Assault**, **L2 Rime Control**, **L3 Gloam Ledger**, or **L4 Prism Weaver**. Read the displayed equipment and 22-card deck before beginning.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the opponent">
    Start with **Greedy**, then try **ISMCTS Low**. **Random Legal** and **ISMCTS High** are also available. ISMCTS Low is the provisional default; the higher search setting may take longer to act.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play the duel">
    Choose whether to keep or return cards during the mulligan. During your turn, inspect the legal **Manifest**, **Veil**, targeting, and end-turn actions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your results">
    After the match, download **the Duel Record** and **the session file**. Keep both together for verified replay and reports.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## During the match

Watch the mana, Ward, health, Charge, status and Schatten-slot panels as you act. The log should explain how an action changed the battle. If a result is unclear, note the exact moment and include your exported duel.

For more detail, read [casting and Veil](/rules/casting), then [replay your duel](/replay/record-and-review).
