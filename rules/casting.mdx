SCHATTEN lets you cast from your hand in two different ways. Learning when to **Manifest** and when to **Veil** is one of the main tactical decisions in each duel.

## Manifest

**Manifest** resolves a card from your hand immediately. Its action becomes visible as it resolves.

## Veil

**Veil** places a card face-down into your leftmost available Schatten slot. Your opponent can see that a slot is occupied and the card's cost and fuse, but not the card identity. A veiled card normally resolves when its fuse reaches zero. A veiled **Reaction** resolves when its trigger occurs.

The public information lets an opponent plan around a threat without necessarily knowing which card is waiting.

## Other signals to watch

- **Mana and carry:** compare current mana, cap, and carry before and after casting and ending turns.
- **Ward, health, statuses and Charge:** watch how these change in the panels and log.
- **Charge at turn end:** when a Charge is active, the interface may offer ending with carry into a slot or allowing it to gutter.
- **Dusk discard:** if your hand exceeds its limit, choose discards or let the engine select them.
- **Targets and choices:** legal targets and options are exposed as separate action buttons.
