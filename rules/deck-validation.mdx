A SCHATTEN deck is generated by the equipment worn by its wizard. The current `proto-b22` format fixes seven slots and a total of **22 cards**.

## Required equipment shape

| Slot | Cards contributed |
| --- | ---: |
| Staff | 6 |
| Focus | 4 |
| Vestment | 4 |
| Hood | 3 |
| Ring I | 2 |
| Ring II | 2 |
| Relic | 1 |
| **Total** | **22** |

Both rings occupy separate slots and must contain distinct item IDs.

## Legality rules

- Exactly one equipped item per slot, with the correct slot type.
- No duplicate equipment item ID.
- All equipped items and their granted cards must be in the approved format pool.
- No item grants more than **2 copies of one card**.
- No deck contains more than **3 copies of one card** overall.
- At most **4 distinct non-neutral disciplines** among deck cards.
- All derived stats must satisfy the legal ranges, with specified clamp warnings where applicable.

## Derived stat ranges

| Stat | Source | Legal range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Max Health | Vestment + modifiers | 20–32 |
| Loom capacity | Staff + modifiers | 3–5 |
| Schatten slots | Hood + modifiers | 1–5 |
| Mana cap | Default 6; some relics constrain it | 4–6 |
| Hand limit | Default 8; some items constrain it | 5–8 |
| Plays per turn | Normally unlimited | No lower than 3 |

Item processing takes a slot base, adds additive modifiers, applies the minimum set modifier, then clamps to the legal range. Invalid Health below 20 is rejected. Other out-of-range effects can produce warnings as specified by the validator.

## Prototype versus final game

The validator already supports equipment-defined deck construction. The **browser prototype currently offers four validated presets**, not a public equipment-inventory editor. The rules here explain why those presets have the cards they do.
