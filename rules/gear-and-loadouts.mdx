Unlike games that begin with freely assembling individual cards, SCHATTEN's design centers on **equipment defining the cards in your deck**. The current browser playtest exposes this idea through fixed validated loadouts.

## The four prototype loadouts

| Preset | Name | How to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L1 | Ember Assault | Select it in the setup screen |
| L2 | Rime Control | Select it in the setup screen |
| L3 | Gloam Ledger | Select it in the setup screen |
| L4 | Prism Weaver | Select it in the setup screen |

Each preset displays equipped items and the resulting **22-card deck** before a duel. The prototype content baseline contains **24 starter card definitions and 19 prototype equipment items**. Those are content definitions, not 24 distinct cards in every single deck.

The opponent can use a fixed preset or a reproducibly selected random loadout. The downloaded session file records the name and selection mode.

## What you cannot do yet

You cannot win gear, maintain an earned inventory, craft an upgraded item, or save custom equipment assemblies through the current browser prototype. Those systems remain future work.

## What to compare now

Play each preset and ask: do you understand the cards the equipment provides? Do you notice meaningful differences between preset strategies? Are any interactions surprisingly confusing or enjoyable?

For authoritative per-card and per-item costs and effects, use the game's setup view until the Codex catalog is generated from validated game content.
