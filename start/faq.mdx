The SCHATTEN prototype focuses on validating its combat rules and player experience, not delivering every system of the final game.

## Can I obtain new gear after a battle?

Not currently. No reward or loot progression loop is present in this prototype.

## How do I upgrade or craft equipment?

Crafting and upgrades are planned ideas, not playable features. You can inspect the existing [equipment library](/items/index) to understand the gear-defined deck concept.

## Can I customize my own deck?

Not through the current browser interface. Choose one of the [four validated equipment presets](/loadouts/index). Each supplies a 22-card deck.

## Where can I see every card?

The setup screen displays cards from the selected loadout. The [full Codex card library](/cards/index) catalogs all 24 prototype card definitions.

## Can I rewatch my match?

Yes. Download the Duel Record, start a new duel, then open the record for verification and turn-by-turn inspection. A session file adds named context but is optional for opening a compatible Duel Record. See [replay](/replay/record-and-review).

## Is cinematic replay already implemented?

No. The browser has a **tactical state replay viewer**. A cinematic battle reconstruction is planned separately.

## Can AI explain my duel JSON?

An AI can infer the winner, action sequence and casting patterns, but the export is not a full readable event transcript. It cannot reliably name each played card or prove optimal alternatives without the matching engine and content. See [AI analysis](/replay/ai-analysis).

## Does the Wizard's Journal sync across devices?

No. Notes live in the current browser. Export them if you need a portable copy.

## What feedback is useful?

Describe exact actions, expected and observed behavior, and the relevant replay step. [Playtest scenarios](/playtest/test-scenarios) help structure tests.
